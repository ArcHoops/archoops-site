Big News

ArcHoops is live for NBA opening night

NBA opening night is October 20, and ArcHoops is live right alongside it. Whether you're joining as a family, a classroom, or a whole school, sign-up is open now—no waiting on anyone else to get started. Every prediction and every piece of feedback from every user, student, and school helps shape what ArcHoops becomes.

Homepage — Season Launch

Every user—students on a class roster or kids signing up at home—now lands on a real leaderboard from their very first prediction, not just once they join a class. Make your first call today, and you're already on the board before NBA opening night.

School Spotlight

Meet our Founding School

Every new thing needs a first believer. For ArcHoops, that's Park Forest Elementary, the school that spent last season testing predictions, lessons, and more than a few bugs so this launch could be ready for everyone else. We're proud to call them our Founding School.

Park Forest Elementary — Pilot Season

Opening Night Classroom Challenge

On October 20, NBA opening night, classes everywhere can predict the score, the winner, and a team stat for one of the games—then review the results together the next morning. It's a fun way for a class to kick off the season as a team, not just as individual players.

Any school that joins before Thanksgiving earns Founding School status, just like Park Forest Elementary. If that's you, or you're a teacher who wants your school to be, email info@archoops.net and we'll get you set up. Founding Schools may find a few perks coming their way this season: stay tuned.
