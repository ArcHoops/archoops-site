School Spotlight

Meet our Founding School

Every new thing needs a first believer. For ArcHoops, that's Park Forest, the school that spent last season testing predictions, lessons, and more than a few bugs so this launch could be ready for everyone else. We're proud to call them our Founding School.

Opening Night Classroom Challenge

On October 20, NBA opening night, classes everywhere can predict the score, the winner, and a team stat for one of the games—then review the results together the next morning. It's a fun way for a class to kick off the season as a team, not just as individual players.

Any school that joins before Thanksgiving earns Founding School status, just like Park Forest. If that's you—or you're a teacher who wants your school to be—info@archoops.net and we'll get you set up.

We're still shaping what Founding School status includes. Ideas on the table include direct access to our team through the season, early looks at new features, and a reward for the top class in the Opening Night Challenge. We'll share the details in an upcoming issue.

Product Update

Smoother for everyone, start to finish

A lot changed behind the scenes to get ready for a full season of real games. A few of the upgrades you'll notice:

What's new

  • A global leaderboard for anyone, class or no class—direct sign-ups now have somewhere to compete from day one
  • Interactive lessons that actually track your progress, so finishing one unlocks the next without a second thought
  • A toggle to switch between NBA and WNBA games
  • A feedback button right in the app—tell us what's working (or isn't) without leaving the page

We're tuning ArcHoops all season with real games and real students. The in-app feedback button is the fastest way to tell us what's working and what to improve.

Built For Every Student

One sign-up experience, for every age

We simplified how we protect students using ArcHoops. Instead of different rules for different ages, every student under 18 now gets the same experience: they can start predicting and using lessons right away, while a parent or guardian is asked by email to approve the account. If we don't hear back within 3 days, the account and its data are automatically deleted—no action needed on your end.

Usernames stay hidden on every leaderboard, so classmates can compete without anyone's identity on display. Nothing here requires a school or district to set up—it works the same whether a student joins through a classroom or signs up with a parent at home.
