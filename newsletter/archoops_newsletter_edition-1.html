A fresh new look and a fuller learning loop

We rebuilt the student experience from the ground up. Beyond a cleaner, easier-to-navigate design, the new interface completes the ArcHoops learning loop with two new components that turn a single prediction into real understanding.

Observe. Watch predictions come alive

Big News

We completed Nexus Louisiana's Startup School

ArcHoops was accepted into Nexus Louisiana, a Baton Rouge tech incubator that connects early-stage founders with mentors, partners, and resources, with a dedicated focus on education. As part of the cohort, we participated in the Stealthmode Startup School, a six-week intensive program built to get first-time founders ready for launch.

Over those six weeks, we worked through legal and IP partnerships, learned startup finance and fundraising fundamentals (SAFEs, cap tables, and managing dilution), refined our pitch-deck strategy, and explored new ways to fold AI into our workflow, experimentation, and research.

Since finishing, we've put those lessons straight to work: refreshing our pitch deck to meet investor-ready standards, finalizing our data room, and reassessing our trademark needs. For our partners and potential investors, it means the version of ArcHoops you meet today is sharper, better-organized, and built to scale.

From the Classroom

Highlights from our pilot with Park Forest Elementary

In our first in-school pilot at Park Forest Elementary, students didn't just play: they reasoned with data. The numbers tell the story.

52students
~1,600predictions
170+games analyzed
85%completed a lesson

Students who completed at least one lesson made predictions with 15% higher accuracy—a clear sign they carried the analytics into their thinking. Teachers observed students using terms like "statistics" on their own and comparing results with classmates. They even had non-participants requesting to join the pilot. For district leaders and educators, these early results point to measurable gains in reasoning and engagement when ArcHoops runs alongside a Tier 1 math curriculum.
