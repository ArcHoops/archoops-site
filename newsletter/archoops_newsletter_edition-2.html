Data science starts with the numbers.

Look at the numbers first. Then make the call.

Thank you for being here. This edition covers what we've built for schools and families alike, a simpler way students meet the data, and an open question we're working through about how to reward good thinking.

Big News

Putting the data where the decision happens

The scouting report is moving to the top of the prediction view. Instead of picking first and seeing the numbers after, students will open a matchup to a clear visual snapshot of a team's scoring patterns and recent history—before they make the call.

Scouting Report — Team Snapshot

Why it matters

  • See how a team has really been playing before you pick, not after
  • Spot patterns and surprises at a glance
  • Put to work the same data-reading skills students build in our lessons

Reading and interpreting data is its own skill, and it's one our lessons teach directly—walking students through how to spot trends, spread, and what actually tends to predict a win. Moving the scouting report to the top in a moment of decision encourages students to put that skill to work right when it counts.

The payoff shows up in the numbers: in our Park Forest pilot, students who completed at least one lesson on reading the data predicted with 15% higher accuracy than those who hadn't.

Product Update

Built to fit your classroom – or your living room

Last issue showed you the new student dashboard. This one is about what surrounds it: whether ArcHoops shows up as a full school rollout or as something a family signs up for at home, getting started should be simple either way.

For schools, the redesigned admin experience handles organization management, bulk email invitations, and account rules to keep enrollment limited to a school's own accounts. A teacher or district administrator can now bring in a full roster in one quick pass.

Teachers and administrators also can toggle between a single classroom and a school-wide view of student performance.

And for families signing up on their own, we've built parental consent right into the flow: a child can dive in right away, but a parent or guardian gets an email to approve the account, and if we don't hear back within a set window, the account and everything in it is automatically deleted. It's safety and privacy built in from the first click. It's all in staging now and launching soon.

What's Next

On deck

We're designing a badging system so students have milestones to chase beyond the leaderboard, finishing the interactive lessons, and getting the redesign into classrooms ahead of the coming season.

We're also exploring a bigger question. In most prediction games, a student who guesses at random and gets lucky scores exactly the same as one who studied the matchup and reasoned it out, which quietly teaches the wrong lesson. We're looking into ways to give kids credit for a well-reasoned prediction, even for the games when it doesn't pan out. If you're an educator, coach, or parent with thoughts on this, we'd love to hear them—info@archoops.net.
